SVEC Transportation Facilities

Sri Vasavi Engineering College (SVEC) provides dedicated transport facilities to support commuting needs of both students and faculty. The college operates a large fleet and follows routes across the district to make daily travel safe and convenient.

Fleet size: A fleet of 70 buses is available for faculty and students to commute between campus and their residences.
  • College buses run on multiple routes to cover students and staff coming from different parts of West Godavari district.
  • Free transportation is provided for faculty commuting from Tadepalligudem.
  • Faculty travelling from places other than Tadepalligudem are eligible for a 50% concession in the bus fee.
  • Transport is also provided for students to attend pool campus drives organized at other colleges.
